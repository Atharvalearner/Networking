| Network | Full Form                 | Coverage           | Real Example                        |
| ------- | ------------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| PAN     | Personal Area Network     | 1–10 meters        | Bluetooth between phone and earbuds |
| LAN     | Local Area Network        | Room, Home, Office | Office network                      |
| CAN     | Campus Area Network       | Multiple buildings | University campus                   |
| MAN     | Metropolitan Area Network | Entire city        | City-wide ISP network               |
| WAN     | Wide Area Network         | Country/World      | Internet, Bank network              |
| GAN     | Global Area Network       | Worldwide          | Global mobile/satellite networks    |
| SAN     | Storage Area Network      | Data center        | Server connected to storage arrays  |


1. PAN: 
Coverage: short-range network 
connect personal devices such as smartphones, smartwatches, wireless keyboards, and Bluetooth headsets.
Technologies: Bluetooth, NFC, USB(wired personal connection)

2. LAN:
connects devices within a limited area such as a home, office, laboratory, or school building.
coverage: few meters to a few kilometers
Technologies: Ethernet, WiFi

3. CAN:
related to the geographical location
connects multiple LANs within a campus or organization or university or hospital.
coverage: 1–5 km (typically)

4. MAN:
connects networks across a city or metropolitan area.
coverage: Entire City
examples: Cable TV Network, Metro Ethernet, Smart City Networks

5. WAN:
connects networks across countries or continents.
coverage: Thousands of kilometers
Internet is the world's largest WAN.
connects geographically distant networks using technologies such as leased lines, MPLS, VPNs, or the Internet.

6. GAN:
a worldwide network that provides communication across multiple countries and continents.
eg: Global satellite phone systems, International mobile networks, Worldwide corporate communication systems
provides worldwide communication by interconnecting WANs using technologies such as satellite and international communication infrastructure.

7. SAN:
dedicated high-speed network used to connect servers with centralized storage for fast, reliable, block-level data access.
Used In: Data Centers, Banks, Cloud Providers, VMware Clusters
Technologies: Fibre Channel, iSCSI, Fibre Channel over Ethernet (FCoE)
